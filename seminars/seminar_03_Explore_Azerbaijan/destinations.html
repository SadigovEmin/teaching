Gabala

Gabala is a popular destination for nature lovers. The area is surrounded by forests and mountains and offers a relaxing escape from city life.

Visitors can enjoy outdoor activities and explore the area's natural scenery.

Activities

  • Nature walks
  • Mountain cable car rides
  • Picnics near the lake

Best season: Spring, summer and autumn

Main Attractions

  • Nohur Lake
  • Tufandag Mountain Resort
  • Gabala forests

Shahdag

Shahdag is known for its mountain resort and snowy winter landscapes. It is a popular destination for adventure and outdoor recreation.

Activities

  • Skiing and snowboarding
  • Cable car rides
  • Mountain walks

Best season: Winter for snow; summer for mountain activities

Main Attractions

  • Shahdag Mountain Resort
  • Mountain trails
  • Panoramic viewpoints

Sheki

Sheki is a historic city with traditional architecture, crafts, rich cuisine and a distinctive cultural atmosphere.

Activities

  • Historic walking tour
  • Visit local craft shops
  • Try Sheki halva

Best season: Spring and autumn

Main Attractions

  • Sheki Khan's Palace
  • Caravanserai
  • Historic city streets

Quba

Quba is a northern region of Azerbaijan associated with forests, rivers, apple orchards and mountain scenery.

Activities

  • Forest walks
  • Photography
  • Local food tasting

Best season: Spring and summer

Main Attractions

  • Qechresh forest
  • Quba city center
  • Mountain roads and viewpoints

Khinalig

Khinalig is a high mountain village known for its remote location, distinctive traditions and dramatic views.

Activities

  • Village walks
  • Mountain photography
  • Learn about local culture

Best season: Late spring and summer

Main Attractions

  • Traditional village houses
  • Mountain panoramas
  • Local cultural heritage

Goygol

Goygol is a natural destination with a lake, forest landscapes and fresh mountain air. It is an ideal place for a quiet day outdoors.

Activities

  • Walking in nature
  • Picnicking in permitted areas
  • Landscape photography

Best season: Late spring, summer and early autumn

Main Attractions

  • Goygol Lake
  • Goygol National Park
  • Nearby forest viewpoints
