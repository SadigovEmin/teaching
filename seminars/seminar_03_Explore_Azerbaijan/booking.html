Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my travel dates?

Contact our team as early as possible and we will check available options.

Are meals included in every tour?

Meal details are listed in each tour package. Please check the comparison table.

Can children join the tours?

Children can join selected tours with a responsible adult. Contact us for details.

What should I pack?

Pack comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, personal medication and a water bottle.

Is travel insurance included?

Insurance is included only in packages where it is listed. You can also request it as an extra service.
